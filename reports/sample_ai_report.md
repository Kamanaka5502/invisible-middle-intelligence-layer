# Sample Invisible Middle Report

## Case

**System reviewed:** AI-supported workplace drafting workflow  
**Claimed benefit:** Faster output generation and operational efficiency  
**Visible completion:** AI-generated document delivered rapidly  
**Primary finding:** AI BURDEN TRANSFER / REBALANCE REQUIRED  
**Evidence strength:** Strong

## Summary

The AI workflow accelerates visible output production while redistributing verification, interpretive monitoring, authority risk, and trust-repair burden into the human operator.

The system appears more efficient on the surface, but the Invisible Middle assessment indicates that substantial afterwork remains hidden inside the workflow.

## Structural Interpretation

The primary structural condition is **AI burden transfer**.

Secondary conditions include:

- trust instability
- authority burden
- closure weakness
- verification dependency

The workflow reduces visible production time while increasing hidden cognitive responsibility.

## Human Compensation Map

### Employee / Operator

- verification burden
- interpretive monitoring
- confidence checking
- authority risk
- trust repair
- output rewrite burden

### Manager / Reviewer

- release uncertainty
- liability concern
- escalation burden

### Organisation

- apparent productivity masking hidden afterwork
- incomplete visibility into verification load

## Burden Domains

Detected burden domains:

- verification burden
- authority burden
- monitoring burden
- trust repair burden
- closure burden
- AI oversight burden

## Flourishing Impact

| Dimension | Reading |
|---|---|
| Confidence | Unstable |
| Cognitive Sustainability | Degraded |
| Workflow Trust | Weakened |
| Participation | Strained |
| Autonomy | Reduced |

## Rebalance Recommendation

Clarify human authority boundaries, improve uncertainty signalling, reduce hidden verification work, strengthen closure confidence, and prevent AI acceleration from redistributing unresolved risk into operators.

## Final Reading

The relevant question is not whether the AI generated output faster.

The relevant question is whether the system reduced effort or merely displaced unresolved consequence management into humans after generation.
