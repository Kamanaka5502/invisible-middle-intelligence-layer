# Sample Invisible Middle Report

## Case

**System reviewed:** Online SEN support-plan portal  
**Claimed benefit:** Streamlined documentation and review  
**Visible completion:** Support plan submitted  
**Primary finding:** DISPLACEMENT / REBALANCE REQUIRED  
**Evidence strength:** Strong

## Summary

The portal creates visible administrative completion, but the Invisible Middle assessment indicates that significant context, verification, authority, and emotional burden remain distributed across the child, parent/carer, teacher, and SENCO.

The system appears organised, but it does not sufficiently hold lived context across review cycles.

## Structural Interpretation

The primary structural condition is **context fragmentation**.

Secondary conditions include:

- ownership drift
- closure weakness
- participation strain
- repeated explanation burden

The system is asking humans to preserve meaning that the infrastructure should retain.

## Human Compensation Map

### Child / Learner

- lived need compressed into administrative fields
- sensory and cognitive burden underrepresented
- participation risk hidden beneath submitted documentation

### Parent / Carer

- repeated explanation
- context repair
- emotional containment
- trust repair
- evidence reconstruction

### Teacher / Practitioner

- translation of lived reality into rigid categories
- workflow repair
- verification burden

### SENCO

- missing context verification
- ownership clarification
- review continuity burden

## Burden Domains

Detected burden domains:

- context reconstruction burden
- interpretive burden
- verification burden
- authority burden
- closure burden
- trust repair burden

## Flourishing Impact

| Dimension | Reading |
|---|---|
| Agency | Reduced |
| Participation | Strained |
| Confidence | Unstable |
| Continuity | Weak |
| Recovery Capacity | Degraded |

## Rebalance Recommendation

Preserve lived context across review cycles, reduce repeated explanation, clarify ownership for continuity, add sensory and cognitive burden indicators, and prevent administrative completion from replacing actual support.

## Final Reading

The system should not be assessed only by whether the support plan was submitted.

The more important question is whether the process reduced burden or moved it into the people least able to refuse it.
