# Field Development Map

This is not a generic feature roadmap.

The Invisible Middle Intelligence Layer does not deepen by adding ordinary product features first.

It deepens by improving how hidden compensation is detected, interpreted, evidenced, and rebalanced across interacting human/system fields.

## Core Field Question

> Where is the system asking humans to compensate for missing structure?

## Development Fields

### 1. Human Signal Field

Purpose:

Detect where unresolved system conditions first become visible through human strain, uncertainty, repeated checking, avoidance, reassurance seeking, navigation looping, overload, or participation loss.

Development direction:

- neurodivergent early-detection patterns
- high-sensitivity interaction signals
- nervous-system friction indicators
- lived context distortion markers
- participation-risk signals

Safeguard:

This field must never become deficit scoring.

The human signal is evidence of system condition, not proof of individual failure.

---

### 2. Burden Field

Purpose:

Map the hidden work being transferred into people.

Development direction:

- cognitive load
- verification load
- authority load
- context-repair load
- trust-recovery load
- closure burden
- monitoring burden
- escalation burden
- AI oversight burden

Core question:

> What is the person carrying that the system should have contained?

---

### 3. Flourishing Field

Purpose:

Assess whether the system expands or degrades human capability, confidence, recovery, trust, and participation.

Development direction:

- agency
- participation
- confidence
- recovery capacity
- relational trust
- continuity
- cognitive sustainability
- healthy autonomy

Core question:

> Does the system leave people more capable, or merely more processed?

---

### 4. System Field

Purpose:

Identify structural conditions inside workflows, AI outputs, forms, governance thresholds, handoff points, and ownership boundaries.

Development direction:

- workflow structure
- AI output conditions
- governance thresholds
- handoff points
- missing ownership
- closure architecture
- predictability conditions
- context continuity

Core question:

> What missing structure is producing the hidden compensation?

---

### 5. Movement Field

Purpose:

Track where burden moves through the system.

Development direction:

- where burden transfers
- where burden accumulates
- where burden rebounds
- where burden compresses
- where burden becomes consequence
- where rebalance is possible

Core question:

> Did the system reduce burden, or move it somewhere less visible?

---

### 6. Evidence Field

Purpose:

Preserve why an interpretation is being made.

Development direction:

- signal families
- cross-domain corroboration
- temporal persistence
- confidence basis
- evidence strength
- reportable finding logic
- structured evidence envelopes

Core question:

> What supports the structural interpretation?

---

### 7. Rebalance Field

Purpose:

Define what restoration looks like.

Development direction:

- trust rebalance
- closure rebalance
- ownership rebalance
- predictability rebalance
- interpretive rebalance
- AI burden rebalance
- context continuity rebalance
- flourishing restoration

Core question:

> What must change before the system can be called smooth, trustworthy, and sustainable?

---

### 8. Optional Boundary Field

Purpose:

Where licensed and appropriate, produce public-safe evidence envelopes that may interface with external governance or runtime systems.

Development direction:

- non-binding surface signals
- evidence-envelope compatibility
- dashboard/report exports
- optional future boundary handoff
- protected-runtime separation

Safeguard:

This public repository does not expose or claim a live protected substrate.

## Development Principle

The layer must not become:

- ordinary monitoring
- employee scoring
- productivity analytics
- UX polish
- wellbeing branding
- generic AI governance
- behavioural surveillance

The layer exists to interpret hidden human compensation as a structural system condition.

The next serious development work should deepen field coherence, not flatten the layer into ordinary software categories.

Priority directions:

1. Strengthen signal taxonomy across education, SEN, AI, workforce, care, public service, and CRM environments.
2. Improve temporal burden trajectory modelling.
3. Add multi-role burden movement tracing.
4. Expand flourishing restoration indicators.
5. Refine evidence strength and confidence-basis logic.
6. Build dashboard surfaces that show field relationships without reducing humans to metrics.
7. Preserve NDPP/neurodivergent-origin integrity across all buyer-facing language.
8. Maintain public/protected boundary discipline around Elyria Systems / VA.
